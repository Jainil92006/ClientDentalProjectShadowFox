Privacy Policy

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and disclose information from visitors and users of our website.

Updated —

Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, and message content when submitted via forms.

Usage Data: Browser type, device information, pages visited, and timestamps for improving our services.

Cookies

We may use essential and preference cookies to enhance user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some functionality may be limited.

How We Use Your Information
  • Operate our website and provide requested services.
  • Respond to inquiries and appointment requests.
  • Improve site performance and user experience.
  • Communicate updates, only with your consent where required.

Data Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Rights
  • Access, update, or delete your personal information, subject to legal limits.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us. We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe.
